Signing in should add things. Not change the app.

Today the app has two of everything: two homes, two game lists, two players, six ways to start an evening. Signing in swaps the simple half for the complicated one. This is the same app with one of each — tap the phone, and use the switch above it to see what a new person, a signed-in person and a paired couple each get from the same screen.

One primary action per screen Exactly one filled button anywhere you look. Everything else is outlined or quiet. Today's home has two loud buttons competing and three more beneath them, which is why nothing tells you what to do.
Signing in enables, never replaces The same home, the same list, the same player, whoever you are. Apart and memories sit there greyed with "set up" rather than living on a different screen you meet later.
One word for one thing Heat is Playful, Hot, Wild — everywhere, including the planner. "Intensity", "chapter" and "experience" stop being words a person reads.
No screen with a void The "your partner has a night ready" card currently renders a question and a thousand pixels of nothing. Every screen shows its answer, or it is not finished.
Ask at the moment, not before An account is offered where it buys something, saying what it buys. Nothing is asked for on the way in except an age and a heat.

What this retires

  • HomeScreen's button wall — replaced by the one home above.
  • GamesScreen — the second game list, with its own card style and "3 ROUNDS" labels.
  • BuildNightScreen's six-field form — becomes "Plan the evening", three questions, behind Start.
  • The chapter player's shape screen, anticipation beats and check-in — the card player covers them.
  • Route.Games, Route.Game, Route.InstantHome, Route.InstantPlay — four routes become two.
  • The five-level intensity ladder as something a person sees. It stays in the engine, where it belongs.
Every button in the phone works. The switch above it changes who is using the app, not which app it is — that is the whole proposal. Nothing here changes the privacy architecture: the server still filters, refusals are still silent, and what a couple said no to still never reaches the other one.
