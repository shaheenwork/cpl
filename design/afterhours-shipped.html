The rebuild, and the four places it argued back.

This is what shipped, at the values in the repo — not the proposal. Every colour, gap and radius below is read from Color.kt, Dimens.kt and Light.kt. Where the implementation forced a change, it says so and why. The heat switch is the argument the whole palette rests on: one setting, and the room changes.

Wild is as dark as contrast allows

The brief asked for oxblood. #A3243B measured 2.74:1 against the scene — a filled button a thumb cannot find. #B02A42 is the deepest red that clears 3:1. Oxblood also cannot carry dark text, so Wild's filled state takes cream. The test set the limit, not a preference.

The bloom had to be normalised

Twelve rings with a squared falloff read as light where ten equal ones banded. But alphas accumulate where layers overlap, so the first attempt came out about three and a half times too bright — a solid pink band, worse than what it replaced. It is divided by the series total now, computed rather than guessed.

Burgundy needed a second tone

#C75C7A as text on the card measures 4.29:1 and fails AA. #DE8AA1 is the same hue lifted until it passes; the original stays the fill. Contrast is not a thing to eyeball on a near-black stack.

Optical sizing is not wired, and the brief said it would be

The shipped playfair_display_variable has a wght axis and no opsz. Claiming it in code would have been a lie in a comment, so it is not claimed.
