Afterhours v2

A private game app for one couple. No accounts — the only identity is this phone, and once paired, the other one. Thirty-four screens, drawn at 390×844 in the app's own palette and type.

1 · Opening

Two taps from launch to a card, so the first screen asks the only two things that can't be deferred — are you both adults, and how far tonight.

2 · Home

The whole product on one screen: how far, who's here, and what to play — with Play Now sized so it's reachable without reading anything above it.

3 · Pairing

The only setup in the app, and it only appears when you choose Apart — six characters, no name, no address, and a pair that ends only when somebody says so.

4 · Playing on one phone

A game of turns has to change hands, so the round opens by saying whose it is — and the hand-off is the one screen in the app with no chrome at all.

5 · Playing apart, on two phones

Same games, different waiting — and the only place a photo or a voice note exists in this app is here, as the answer to a dare that asked for proof.

6 · Money

A locked level is answered where it was tapped, with what's inside and what it costs — no countdown, no crossed-out price, no second screen between wanting it and having it.

7 · Settings

One list, and nothing in it that looks like an account — the pair is a fact about two phones, and unpairing is the only thing here that touches the other one.

8 · Safety

Stop asks nothing and argues with nobody; it is the one control in the app that never takes the heat colour, because "end this now" must not read as decoration.
