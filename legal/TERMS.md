You must be **18 or over**, and the person you pair with must be 18 or over. You confirm this before

Afterhours is for two consenting adults in a relationship with each other. It is not a dating app, not
a social network, and not a place to reach strangers.

## 2. What Afterhours is

An app that suggests things for two partners to do together or apart, built around what you have both
privately said you are curious about, and kept inside the limits each of you sets. It suggests; you
decide. Nothing in the app is advice — medical, psychological, legal or otherwise.

## 3. Consent, and stopping

- Everything in the app is a suggestion. You are free to skip anything, to say "not this one", and to

  no reason.
- A limit you set is honoured. An evening is built inside the limits of **both** of you, and the app
  will not offer content above the lower of your two levels.
- Consent between you and your partner is yours to give and to withdraw, in the moment, whatever the
  app suggests. Neither the app nor your earlier answers commit you to anything.
- If something in the app is wrong for you, report it: four taps, no free text, and your partner is

## 5. Pairing, and unpairing

Pairing needs both people: a code from one, entered by the other, and then an approval on the first
person's own device, with matching symbols shown to both. Either of you can unpair at any time, from
Settings, without the other's permission. Unpairing ends the couple and what it held.

Deleting your account deletes the couple and everything under it, including things you made together.
The other person keeps their own account and their own private answers. The app says this plainly
before you tap, and the real button is behind a second one. It cannot be undone.
